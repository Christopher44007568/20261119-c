NUMERICAL NOTES / 006

What is being solved?

The editor computes bounded, partition-of-unity biharmonic influence fields on the polygon minus its interior void.

01

The domain is sampled on a Cartesian lattice. Lattice points inside the void are discarded; they cannot contribute a stencil value.

02

A masked 13-point finite-difference Δ² operator acts only on valid samples. Free boundaries use zero normal flux.

03

Each handle imposes a Dirichlet column. At every free sample the four weights are projected onto the simplex and box to enforce bounds and Σw = 1.

04

Residuals audit the actual displayed field. A coincident-handle or out-of-domain constraint is infeasible and is reported instead of being relaxed silently.
